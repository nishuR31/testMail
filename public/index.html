Providers & load balancing

Each new email goes to the selected provider with the shortest estimated wait, (pending + 1) / requests-per-second. An empty queue takes the next job, a busy one is skipped, and an email that fails permanently is failed over to another selected provider. Providers out of free-tier quota are skipped.
